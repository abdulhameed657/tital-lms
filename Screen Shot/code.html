Founder & Owner

With over two decades of experience in both structural engineering and educational technology, Alexander Sterling founded Titan LMS to bridge the divide between theoretical knowledge and professional excellence.

Under his ownership, Titan has grown from a specialized architectural training tool into a global leader in high-performance learning management systems, serving the world's most demanding institutions.

The Titan Story

Titan began not in a boardroom, but on a construction site. Our founder noticed that even the most talented engineers struggled to translate their classroom learning to the complexities of real-world precision projects.

He saw a need for a "Titan" class of software—something robust, unbreakable, and capable of supporting the heaviest weights of academic ambition. What started as a bespoke training module for elite engineering firms evolved into a comprehensive LMS that treats learning like a mission-critical operation.
